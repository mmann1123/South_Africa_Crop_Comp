## 3. Where the Introduction and Related Work lack 2023-2026 cross-region / domain-generalization coverage (AE request)

### Takeaway
The only post-2022 transfer citations are ma2024 (review), cropformer2023, mai2025, pankajakshan2025 (hyperspectral) and tufail2025cnnrnn (not a transfer paper). No citation covers 2023-2026 domain generalization or unsupervised domain adaptation for crop mapping in China, Europe or the Americas, or SITS foundation-model pre-training. There are six natural insertion points, listed below.

### Cited Findings
These are based on reading the manuscript text (cea-article.tex, lines given). No external source is needed for locating them.

- **A:59, sentence "yet studies that re-rank models under a spatially disjoint holdout remain rare \citep{pankajakshan2025, jin2019, rustowicz2019}"**. This is the primary insertion point for 2023-2026 crop-type studies that evaluate on spatially disjoint regions or tiles: cross-region benchmarks, and leave-region-out evaluations in Europe (e.g., studies on EuroCrops/BreizhCrops-style multi-region data), China, and the U.S. (CDL-based cross-state transfer). It also lets the paper soften "rare" to "less common", given that such studies exist.
- **A:59, sentence "the bulk of the transfer-learning literature, which adapts a model using target-domain data: labels, unlabeled imagery, or self-supervised pre-training \citep{ma2024, cropformer2023, mai2025}"**.
