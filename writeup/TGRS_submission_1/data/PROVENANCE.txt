South Africa Crop Classification — processed dataset (IEEE TGRS companion)
================================================================================

These files are DERIVED products built from three upstream sources. They are not
the original challenge data; they are the processed, model-ready form used in the
paper. Please cite all upstream sources (CITATION.txt) and review the terms below
before reuse or redistribution.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. UPSTREAM SOURCES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) GROUND-TRUTH LABELS / FIELD BOUNDARIES
    Western Cape Department of Agriculture & Radiant Earth Foundation (2021),
    "Crop Type Classification Dataset for Western Cape, South Africa," Version 1.0,
    Radiant MLHub.  DOI: https://doi.org/10.34911/rdnt.j0co8q  License: CC-BY-4.0.
    This dataset underlies both the Radiant Earth "Spot the Crop" challenge and the
    AI4FoodSecurity (South Africa) challenge (ESA, with Planet, TUM, DLR, Radiant
    Earth). Field polygons were digitized from sub-meter aerial photography and
    carry a single seasonal crop label per field.
    -> Source of: crop_id, crop_name, field geometries, fid, SHAPE_AREA/SHAPE_LEN.

(b) SATELLITE IMAGERY
    Copernicus Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface reflectance, extracted via Google Earth
    Engine for the Jan-Dec 2017 season, cloud-masked with s2cloudless plus a shadow
    mask, and composited monthly (6 features/month: bands B2, B6, B11, B12, plus
    EVI and Hue). June had no usable imagery; May was discarded for cloud.
    -> Source of: the raw band-month values in merged_dl_*.parquet and the patch
       parquets.

(c) TIME-SERIES FEATURES
    xr_fresh (open source, MIT) — automated time-series feature extraction.
    https://github.com/mmann1123/xr_fresh
    -> Source of: the 174 xr_fresh statistics in final_data.parquet and
       combined_test_features.parquet.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW THESE FILES WERE PRODUCED (summary; full pipeline in the code repo)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Google Earth Engine extraction + monthly compositing  ->  per-band rasters
  -> zonal/pixel sampling within field polygons        ->  merged_dl_*.parquet
  -> xr_fresh feature extraction per pixel/field        ->  final_data.parquet,
                                                            combined_test_features.parquet
  -> 100 m patch tiling + resampling                    ->  patch_level_data.parquet,
                                                            test_patch_data.parquet
Reproduce from raw inputs with the numbered scripts (0_create_training_data.py,
3_create_test_data.py) in https://github.com/mmann1123/South_Africa_Crop_Comp.
