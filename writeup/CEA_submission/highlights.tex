\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Sparse, axis-aligned feature selection, not deep capacity, drives spatial transfer.
  \item A sparse-attention encoder (L-TAE-S) ties TabNet for best transfer, trains faster.
  \item Field-level aggregation is a second, substitutable lever for robust transfer.
  \item Model rankings invert between in-region and spatially disjoint holdout tests.
  \item Crop models for new farmland must be evaluated across regions, not within one.
\end{itemize}
